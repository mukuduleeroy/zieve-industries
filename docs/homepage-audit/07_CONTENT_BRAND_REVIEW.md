## Media and typography

Original logo pixels are retained, not recreated using text. Industrial visuals are code-authored schematics, consistently identified by visible placeholder captions and appropriate alternative descriptions; none is presented as a company facility, inventory photograph or completed project. This truthful provenance is a strength, while lack of approved photography is a final-design dependency (AUD-03). No unlicensed stock photography was introduced by the audit.

Noto Serif family identification is verified from prior PDF metadata, but complete licensed webfont delivery is pending. Actual Chromium platform font was Georgia-Bold at the sampled hero nodes (AUD-04). The footer brief's reference to existing sans-serif navigation conflicts with the implemented/approved serif stack; retaining the documented stack is consistent with DESIGN.md. No unrelated font/colour system was found.
