# Final readiness assessment

**Recommendation: NO-GO for public launch.** The homepage is suitable for controlled stakeholder review and for planning an explicitly approved Phase 11. No audit fixes are implemented.

1. **Overall:** Complete initial homepage architecture and interactions are present. The foundation is credible, but brand delivery and release paths remain unfinished. Passing regression tests do not supersede the findings.
2. **Visual:** Shared navy/amber palette, alignment and editorial typography establish a coherent corporate direction. Repeated schematics, fallback type and long tablet rhythm keep it below the requested finished cinematic standard. AUD-03/04/07/16 distinguish dependencies from subjective recommendations.
3. **Accessibility:** Good landmark/heading structure, visible focus, native controls, inert hidden content and described validation. Root-font enlargement breaks header/hero fit (AUD-01). Decorative contrast and draft generic labelling need adjudication/refinement (AUD-05/12). Assistive-technology/native-browser validation is incomplete (AUD-09). No conformance claim.
4. **Performance:** Six local placeholder sessions observed no layout-shift entries; median browser LCP was 60ms unthrottled desktop / 596ms simulated mobile. These are local diagnostics, not production CWV or a Lighthouse score. Final fonts/media, client payload and slower-device responsiveness require new measurement (AUD-10).
5. **Function:** Fresh lint, strict TypeScript, production build, 38 unit and 112 Playwright tests pass. Keyboard, quote anchor, solution/accordion, reduced motion and top return operate in Chromium. Major discovery links lead to reserved pages; they are technically valid and publicly incomplete (AUD-02).
6. **Content:** Identity/contact, divisions/pillars and mission/vision/values match the source or approved editorial adaptations. No invented achievements/metrics were found. Illustrations are honestly labelled. Address locality needs confirmation (AUD-14); current business availability was not independently verified.
7. **Release blockers:** Resolve enlarged-text loss; decide useful public discovery destinations within approved scope; approve and deliver final photography/typography or explicitly revise the release brief. Complete native/assistive verification before an accessibility approval. Current noindex/status-route configuration must be deliberately reviewed before public launch. No P0 defect was established; the P1 findings are sufficient to withhold launch approval.
8. **Prioritized improvements:** Address AUD-01/02 first; secure AUD-03/04 assets; then AUD-05/06/12 contrast/form/group semantics; review AUD-07/08 journey/handoff with users; complete AUD-09/10 validation; refresh current docs (AUD-11). Every issue includes evidence, impact, fix recommendation, complexity and regression risk in [the backlog](09_PRIORITIZED_FIX_BACKLOG.md).
9. **Brand dependencies:** Approved rights-cleared industrial photography and mobile/desktop crops; complete licensed Noto Serif regular/bold/italic WOFF2; approved vector/alternate logo masters. Original logo is correctly preserved; vector delivery is a smaller refinement than missing photography/type. Confirm the source address spelling.
10. **Next decision:** Review this audit and approve a concrete Phase 11 scope before changing production code. Do not infer authority to build inner/legal pages, add delivery infrastructure, replace assets or redesign approved sections from this audit. Retest after fixes/final assets. Public release requires a separate readiness decision.

The reports do not assign an unsupported passing score. Verified defects, known development constraints, external dependencies, testing gaps and subjective art-direction recommendations are identified separately. Source integrity evidence confirms the audit-only boundary.
