## Verification standard and limitations

This is a source/file/intended-use record, not legal advice, a model/property-release certificate or blanket permission for future uses. Each production file was traced from its individual original publisher page to its linked licence and download, decoded at native resolution, visually inspected, hashed and assessed against the actual planned generic website use. Direction approval is recorded separately from licence conditions and final visual acceptance. No watermark/paid preview, Google result, AI image or publisher PDF image is shipped.

[Pexels detailed terms](https://www.pexels.com/terms-of-service/) §5 grants downloading, use, copying and adaptation for commercial/noncommercial work subject to its prohibited uses. §6 makes attribution optional. Crucially, §5 restricts commercial use of content depicting brands in relation to goods/services and disclaims independently obtained additional consents. That stricter text was reviewed alongside the [short licence page](https://www.pexels.com/license/); the summary alone was not treated as clearance. This is why PH03 and PH09 are held despite creative direction approval. No assumption that old photographs are CC0 was made from their publication date.

[Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license) allows free commercial downloading and modification, with optional attribution, excluding competing image services/unchanged resale. [Unsplash terms](https://unsplash.com/terms) §5 excludes separate rights in recognizable people, trademarks and artwork. The specific PH05 and PH07-B pages explicitly link the standard licence, not a sponsored paid/Unsplash+ item; downloads match the listed subjects/native files. Both intended uses avoid identifiable people/brands/artwork and add accurate context.

All integrated photos retain visible contributor/publisher credits in their existing figure captions, although those platform licences do not require attribution. Captions expressly say “Illustrative stock photo”; alt text describes the object and disclaims Zieve ownership/staff/inventory as applicable. This does not turn the source into Zieve-owned imagery or authorize an endorsement. No permissions were requested on the user's behalf.
