ELUTION PLANTS & GOLD PROCESSING   ENGINEERED RECOVERY
This is where Zieve Industries’ technical credibility is most visible. With Zimbabwe’s gold sector — and particularly its 
rapidly formalising Artisanal and Small-Scale Mining (ASM) segment — driving national export earnings, the demand for 
efficient, well-engineered gold-recovery infrastructure has never been higher. We supply and support complete elution 
plants designed to maximise recovery and deliver high-purity gold output.
Understanding the Process
In modern gold recovery, ore is leached using a cyanide solution, and the dissolved gold-cyanide complex is adsorbed onto 
activated carbon within a Carbon-in-Leach (CIL) or Carbon-in-Pulp (CIP) circuit. The “loaded” carbon — now carrying the 
gold — is transferred to the elution circuit, the heart of the recovery plant. There the gold is stripped (desorbed) from the 
carbon using a hot caustic-cyanide solution, producing a concentrated pregnant eluate. That solution is passed to 
electrowinning cells, where gold is deposited onto cathodes, before final smelting into doré bars.
Our elution plants are engineered around proven desorption technologies — including the AARL (split-elution) and Zadra 
processes — and incorporate the complete circuit the recovery chain depends on:
Acid wash columns to remove carbonate and inorganic foulants from loaded carbon.
Elution (desorption) columns with integrated heating for efficient gold stripping.
Electrowinning cells for high-yield gold deposition.
Carbon regeneration kilns to restore carbon activity and extend working life, protecting consumable cost.
We offer plants across a range of batch capacities — typically configured from compact 0.5-tonne to 1-tonne carbon-per-
batch units suited to ASM cooperatives and pilot operations, scaling up to 2-tonne-plus circuits for established mid-tier 
producers. This scalability allows a small operation to start lean and grow its processing capacity in step with its production.
The operational payoff is direct: a well-specified elution circuit maximises gold recovery, minimises processing losses, and 
delivers the high-purity output that translates into stronger realised value at the point of sale — a decisive advantage for 
miners formalising and optimising their operations.
